KPI makes physical interaction promptable through an explicit interaction contract and online adaptation of the arm controller, while leaving the whole-body tracker unmodified. From a single instruction, a vision-language agent specifies both motion and contact behavior to hoist another robot with a winch, open a door, and carry a box.
