KPI is a general real-time interaction kernel. Placing it on top of a humanoid whole-body tracker unlocks diverse contact-rich loco-manipulation while preserving the tracker's general-purpose tracking capabilities. Paired with a powerful agent such as GPT-6 Astra, the resulting system turns high-level instructions into physical interaction.
